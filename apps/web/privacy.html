Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 24, 2026 • REVISION: V1.0.0

01 // CORE PRIVACY GUARANTEE

Netflix Quick Blur is engineered with an absolute privacy-first philosophy. Your streaming habits, viewing catalog, and settings are strictly client-side.

SUMMARY: Netflix Quick Blur does NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share ANY personal information, browsing history, video titles, IP addresses, or telemetry. It operates 100% locally in your browser sandbox.

02 // DATA EXCLUSION POLICY

  • NO PERSONAL IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • NO BROWSING OR CATALOG TRACKING: Zero logging of movies, shows, genres, or search queries.
  • NO EXTERNAL TELEMETRY: Zero third-party trackers (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry).
  • NO NETWORK CALLS: Content scripts execute with 0 outgoing fetch or XMLHttpRequest routines.

03 // PERMISSION ENCLAVE

PERMISSION: STORAGE

Used strictly to persist your local blur settings (blur radius, dimmer percentage, hotkey, and active presets) via Chrome's native sandboxed storage API.

HOST PERMISSION: HTTPS://WWW.NETFLIX.COM/*

Required solely to inject the control button into the Netflix playback bar and apply CSS filter styles to the active video player element on netflix.com.

04 // SOURCE AUDITABILITY

The complete source code for Netflix Quick Blur is fully open-source and publicly auditable on GitHub at github.com/TryOmar/netflix-quick-blur.
