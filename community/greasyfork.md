# 🎬 Netflix Quick Blur — Instant Native-Feeling Privacy

Instant, native-feeling privacy, blur & dimming controls for Netflix. Places an elegant Eye button inside Netflix's native playback control bar next to the Fullscreen button, plus an instant keyboard shortcut (**B**).

---

### ✨ Features
- **Native Player Bar Button**: Injected directly alongside the Fullscreen button. Matches Netflix's native control ergonomics.
- **Spacebar Play/Pause Preserved**: Automatically releases button focus (`btn.blur()`) so pressing Spacebar always plays/pauses.
- **Instant Hotkey (B)**: Toggle blur anywhere in playback, even when controls are auto-hidden.
- **Subtitle Dialogue Concealment**: Automatically hides `.player-timedtext` subtitles while blurred to prevent plot spoilers.
- **Tampermonkey Menu Commands**:
  - Toggle Blur
  - Select Presets (*Light Haze*, *Standard Veil*, *Total Blackout*)
  - Toggle Mute on Blur
  - Toggle Subtitle Concealment
- **Zero Dependencies**: Pure vanilla JavaScript compiled directly from our open-source monorepo.
